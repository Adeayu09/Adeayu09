### 👩‍🔬 About Me / Tentang Saya

I am a **Master's graduate and current Ph.D. Candidate in Environmental Engineering** at **Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology (ITS)**, currently working as a **Researcher at National Taiwan University (NTU)**. My research centers around **Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) using waste-derived biochar**, alongside atmospheric numerical modeling (**WRF-Chem, AERMOD, CALPUFF, HYSPLIT**) for air quality protection and climate change mitigation.

Saya adalah **Lulusan Magister dan Kandidat Ph.D. di bidang Teknik Lingkungan** di **Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)** serta **Peneliti di National Taiwan University (NTU)**. Riset utama saya berfokus pada **penyerapan karbon (carbon capture) menggunakan biochar dari limbah organik** dan pemodelan kualitas udara & cuaca.

---

### 🔬 Core Research Focus / Fokus Riset

- 🍃 **Biochar & Carbon Capture**: Pyrolysis optimization of agricultural & domestic sewage sludge waste for permanent CO₂ sequestration.
- 💨 **Air Quality & Weather Modeling**: Atmospheric numerical simulations of wildfire smoke and industrial emissions using **WRF-Chem**, **WRF-ARW**, **AERMOD**, **CALPUFF**, and **HYSPLIT**.
- 📊 **Spatial Data Science & Remote Sensing**: Evaluating satellite precipitation products (GSMaP, CHIRPS, IMERG) & tropospheric ozone dynamics using Python, MATLAB, QGIS, and ArcGIS.
- 📜 **Policy & Environmental Consulting**: Development of Air Quality Protection and Management Plans (RPPMU) & AMDAL documents.
